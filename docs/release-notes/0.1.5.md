The stable candidate also adds a prepared aggregate availability investigation,
distinguishes missing policy-admitted paths from real file-access denials in the
updated Client, and shows the exact connector and receiver pinned by a flow.
The source validation gate uses Slopwatch 0.5.0 with a reviewed v2 baseline.

Live results can be saved while the original authorized stream is attached and
NetClaw supplies the supported evidence. Reattaching on the current daemon does
not provide authoritative replay for every previous turn, approval or child
result. The app reports an unconfirmed outcome when it cannot establish it.
Provider stalls and post-fix production reliability require separate testing.

Use the [hosted release workflow](../deployment/RELEASES.md) to verify the
archive checksums, SBOMs, Client targets and exact image digests. The coordinated
RatelDesk version is 0.1.3.
