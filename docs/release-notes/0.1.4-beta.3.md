# NetRatel 0.1.4-beta.3

AI Chat and AI Ops can continue an existing ready NetClaw 0.28.2 session using its advertised text-admission version 1, without waiting for the joined event that daemon omits on an initialized attachment. Reattachment never implies completion of an unresolved message.

Recovery persists protocol limitations, cancellation and connection failures with the last attempt and a safe next action. It preserves useful attached streams, retires unused owners, and deduplicates repeated notices and restored excerpts. Live recovered terminal events require the expected native turn number; later console completions cannot stand in for the original message. The shared conversation surface keeps recovery results visible while running or awaiting approval and explains why a healthy daemon connection can still miss conversation output.

The current daemon does not supply durable per-message historical outcomes or replay on every attachment. Exact recovery after a later console turn, complete long-response replay, and prompt ownership-loss notifications remain blocked on the deferred daemon contract in [#475](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/netratel/issues/475). No uncertain message, tool, job or approval is automatically replayed. AI completion does not establish operational success. Historical evidence for [#463](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/netratel/issues/463) and [#474](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/netratel/issues/474) is preserved.

The source repository also includes 39 [dotnet-skills](../integrations/dotnet-skills.md) with their upstream revision, supporting references and MIT license, plus routing guidance in `AGENTS.md`.

Focused source regressions and hosted distribution integrity checks are separate from runtime acceptance. The next-beta retest against the owner's actual paired daemon remains pending; record both served versions/revisions before assessing the remaining contract work. No production deployment or recovery of historical operator sessions is claimed by publication.

Addresses the NetRatel-side work in [#474](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/netratel/issues/474).
