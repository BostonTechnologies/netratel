[#282](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/netratel/issues/282). Release availability
and distribution verification are determined by the immutable release publication
record. Owner deployment and the protected native campaign remain separate acceptance.

digits); unsupported precision is rejected before acceptance.

remain in place. Existing operational-input autofill remediation and
`JobsAutofillAcceptanceTests` supply the saved-dummy positive control; deployed-form
campaign evidence still belongs to the owner stage.
